# YouTube description: Episode 001

**Title (recommended):** Who Really Pays for Free Returns?

---

You click "return," print nothing, pay nothing, and the refund shows up. But the jacket's trip back still has to be paid for: shipping, handling, inspection, lost resale value, sometimes fraud. In this episode we follow one (illustrative) jacket backwards through the returns system and look at who ends up carrying the cost: the retailer, a marketplace seller, the logistics partners, the shopper… or nobody, because the item is written off.

No single answer fits every store. The cost doesn't disappear; companies decide how to absorb it, share it, or pass it along.
