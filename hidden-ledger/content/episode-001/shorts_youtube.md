**YouTube title:** Where does your returned jacket actually go? #Shorts

**Description:**

Back into stock, open-box, repaired, liquidated, donated or recycled. A 2025 study in the Journal of Business Logistics says more than $800 billion of returned and excess inventory moves through secondary markets every year.

Source: Rogers, Z. S., et al. (2025), Journal of Business Logistics, https://doi.org/10.1111/jbl.70014
Full episode: Who Really Pays for Free Returns? (linked above)

#Shorts #returns #liquidation #ecommerce
