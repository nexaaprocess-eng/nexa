## Description (copy from here)

You click "return," pay nothing, and the refund shows up. But the trip back still has to be paid for: shipping, handling, inspection, lost resale value, sometimes fraud. We follow one (illustrative) jacket backwards through the returns system and look at who actually carries the cost: the retailer, a marketplace seller, the logistics companies, the shopper… or nobody, when the item is written off.

There's no single answer for every store. The cost doesn't disappear: companies decide how to absorb it, share it, or pass it along.
