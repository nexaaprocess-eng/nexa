## Description (copy from here)

Music, streaming, cloud storage, fitness, productivity, an AI assistant… none of them feels expensive on its own. So why does almost every app now want a monthly payment instead of a one-time purchase, and who actually comes out ahead?

We go back to when software came in a box, follow Adobe's 2013 switch to subscriptions through its own annual reports, and break down the numbers subscription businesses live by: monthly recurring revenue, churn, retention, customer acquisition cost and lifetime value. Then we look at what data from more than 115,000 subscription apps shows about who wins, who doesn't, and why most subscriptions don't last. Finally, we look at where the model breaks: failed payments, hard-to-cancel sign-ups, and why recurring revenue is not the same as profit.
